6. Accuracy

Positions come from consumer grade phone GPS and from third party AIS data. Both can be wrong, stale, missing, or misleading.

  • A vessel's AIS transponder can be switched off legitimately, and often is in

high risk areas.

  • Satellite and terrestrial AIS coverage has gaps, particularly mid ocean.
  • A phone can lose signal, run out of battery, or be left in a cabin.
  • Estimated arrival times are entered by a person and change constantly.

Silence does not mean something is wrong, and a stale position does not mean a vessel has stopped. The single most common reason for both is an ordinary poor satellite link.
