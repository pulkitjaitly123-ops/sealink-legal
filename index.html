SeaLink

A low bandwidth app for merchant navy crews and the families waiting for them. See where the ship is, which port it reaches next, and a one tap check in, all built to survive a metered satellite link.

SeaLink is not a distress service and is not monitored. In an emergency use your vessel's distress equipment and your company's procedures.
